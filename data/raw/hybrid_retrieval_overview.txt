Hybrid retrieval combines lexical keyword matching with semantic similarity.
BM25 rewards documents that contain important query terms, especially rare
terms. Vector retrieval compares document embeddings and can find passages
with related meaning even when the wording is different. Reciprocal Rank
Fusion combines the ranked lists without assuming their scores share a scale.

Retrieval-augmented generation first retrieves relevant document chunks. An
answer generator uses those chunks as evidence and should cite the source
documents. This process reduces unsupported answers and lets users verify the
information returned by the system.
